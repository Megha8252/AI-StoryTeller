Sham dhal chuki thi. Aasmaan par narangi aur deep purple rang ka ek ajeeb sa mel tha, jaise din aur raat ek doosre se aakhri baar gale mil rahe hon. Arabian Sea ki leherein shor machaate hue kinare se takra rahi theen. Shore par door-door tak koi nahi tha, sirf Kabir aur uske andar chalta hua ek behisaab toofan.

Pichhle do saal Kabir ke liye kisi dhuen ke baadal jaise rahe the. Corporate job ka suffocating pressure, ek toota hua rishta jisme usne apna sab kuch daanv par laga diya tha, aur wo unkahe lafz jo wo kabhi keh nahi paaya. Shehar ki bheed mein rehte hue bhi wo itna akela mehsoos karta tha ki kabhi-kabhi saans lena bhi ek task lagta tha. Har waqt har cheez ko control karne ki koshish—circumstances ko, emotions ko, logon ke opinions ko—ne uske wajood ko nichod kar rakh diya tha.

Aaj, bina kisi plan ke, wo bus pakad kar is anjaan coastal town mein aa gaya tha.

Kabir ne apne jhoote ret par hi chhod diye aur aage badhne laga. Thandi, geeli ret uske pairon ke tale dhasne lagi. Paani pehle uske ankhon (ankles) tak aaya. Thandak aisi thi ki shareer mein ek jhurjhuri daud gayi, par wo ruka nahi. Ek-ek kadam ke saath wo samandar ki gehrai mein utarta gaya.

Knee-deep... waist-deep... aur phir chest-deep. 

Cold ocean water uski saanson ko tez kar raha tha. Paani ka pressure uske seene par mehsoos ho raha tha, bilkul waise hi jaise uski life ka bojh roz uspar dabaav banata tha. Samandar mein ek ajeeb si taqat thi—unforgiving, vast, aur brutally honest. Yahan uski corporate achievements, uski cars, uske regrets—kisi ki koi keemat nahi thi. Samandar ke aage wo bilkul chhota, ek microscopic particle jaisa tha.

Aur tab, ek bohot badi lehar uski taraf aayi. Aam taur par insaan dar ke peeche hat jaata hai ya lehar ke upar koodne ki koshish karta hai. Par Kabir nahi hila. 

Usne apni aankhein dheere se band keen. Apna sar thoda peeche jhuka kar aasmaan ki taraf kiya, aur apne dono haath paani se bahar nikaal kar dono taraf phaila diye—completely outstretched, jaise koi parinda azaad hawa mein apne pankh khol deta hai.

Uski palms upar ki taraf theen, open and unguarded. Koi defence nahi, koi resistance nahi. Yeh ek complete surrender ka gesture tha. 

"Main thak chuka hoon," usne hawa mein phusphusaaya. "Main sab control nahi kar sakta. Take it all."

Lehar uske seene se aakar takraayi. Paani ka ek bada jhoka uske chehre, uske baalon aur uske phaili hui baazuon ke upar se nikal gaya. Par Kabir gira nahi. Waves aayi aur guzar gayi. Uske outstretched haathon ke beech se thandi namkeen hawa beh rahi thi. 

Us lamhe mein, Kabir ko ek ajeeb sa revelation hua. Surrender ka matlab kamzori ya haar maanna nahi hota. Surrender ka matlab hota hai us ladai ko chhod dena jo ladne laayak hi nahi thi. Jab aap apne haath khol dete hain, tabhi aap kuch naya receive kar sakte hain. Jab tak mutthi band thi, tab tak sirf dard aur purani yaadein pakad rakhi theen.

Paani abhi bhi thanda tha, leherein abhi bhi unchaahi theen, par Kabir ke andar ka toofan achanak shaant ho gaya tha. Uske phaili hui baazuon par ab bojh nahi, balki azaadi mehsoos ho rahi thi. Uski band aankhon se ek aansu nikla aur gaalon se fisal kar samandar ke namak mein mil gaya. Us aansu ke saath, mahino purana guilt aur guilt ka wazan hamesha ke liye beh gaya.

Uske chehre par mahino baad ek sachhi, befikr muskaan aayi. 

Usne dheere se apni aankhein kholi. Aasmaan mein pehla sitara chamak raha tha. Usne apne haath niche kiye, paani ko halka sa touch kiya jaise shukriya keh raha ho, aur wapas kinare ki taraf mud gaya. 

Leherein abhi bhi waise hi chal rahi theen, par jo aadmi ab paani se bahar nikal raha tha, wo wo Kabir nahi tha jo andar gaya tha. Wo halka tha, zinda tha, aur azaad tha.
