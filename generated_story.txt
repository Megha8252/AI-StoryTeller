Suraj dhal raha tha, aur aasmaan ne deep orange aur violet ke shades pehan rakhe the. Beach par door-door tak koi nahi tha—sirf Kabir tha, aur uske pairon ke neeche se phisalti hui thandi, geeli ret. 

Pichhle teen saal Kabir ke liye kisi suffocating tunnel jaise rahe the. Corporate deadlines, expectations ka endless pressure, aur ek rishta jo itni khamoshi se toota tha ki uski aawaaz kisi ne suni hi nahi. Mumbai ke un chaar-diwari wale flat mein rehte hue Kabir bhool hi chuka tha ki khul kar saans lena kaisa lagta hai. Wo roz bas jeene ka natak kar raha tha, andar se bilkul khokhla.

Lekin aaj, wo bas chala aaya tha. Bag pack kiya aur train pakad kar Gokarna ke is anjaan, sunsan kinare par khada tha.

Usne apne joote bohot pehle hi nikaal kar ret par phenk diye the. Usne apni jeans ko fold karne ki zahmat bhi nahi uthayi. Wo seedha paani ki taraf chalne laga. 

Pehli wave jab uske pairon se takrayi, toh paani ki thandak ne uski reedh ki haddi mein ek ajeeb si sihran daudayi. Ek aisi thandak jisne uske thake hue jism ko jhinjhod kar jaga diya. Lekin wo ruka nahi. Wo aage badhta gaya. 

Kadam dar kadam, paani gehra hota gaya. Pehle ankles, phir ghutne, aur phir waist. Arabian Sea ka namkeen paani uski shirt ko bhigo chuka tha, kapde uski skin se chipak rahe the. Samundar ka current strong tha, har wave aakar uski body ko peeche dhakelne ki koshish karti, lekin Kabir jam kar khada raha. Ajeeb baat ye thi ki paani jitna gehra ho raha tha, Kabir ke seene ka wazan utna hi halka lag raha tha.

Wo tab tak chalta raha jab tak paani uske chest tak nahi pahunch gaya.

Wahan, beecho-beech lehron ke aagosh mein, Kabir ruk gaya. 

Saamne sirf anant samundar tha—endless, majestic aur fearless. Hawa uske chehre se takra rahi thi, aur paani ki boondein uske chehre ko bura bura bhigo rahi thi. Usne apni aankhein band kar li. Uske kaano mein sirf ek hi aawaaz thi—lehron ka shor, jo kisi purane mantra ki tarah lag raha tha. 

Aur phir, Kabir ne dheere se apne dono hath uthaye. 

Dono baazu poori tarah se hawa mein phail gaye—arms wide open, jaise wo poore universe ko ek gale lagane ki koshish kar raha ho. Ya shayad, khud ko poori tarah se surrender kar raha ho.

Us pose mein, us lamhe mein, Kabir ne ladna band kar diya tha. 

Usne accept kar liya ki sab kuch uske control mein nahi ho sakta. Usne release kar diya wo guilt jo wo saalon se dho raha tha—the fear of not being enough, the grief of losing people, aur wo saare unsaid words jo uske gale mein atke hue the. Jab uske hath us aazaad aasmaan ke neeche faile, toh aisa laga jaise uske kandhon par jo pahaad jaisa bojh tha, wo samundar ki lehron ne ek hi jhatke mein apne andar kheench liya ho.

Kabir ke gaalon par aansu behne lage. Wo garam aansu thande samundar ke paani mein ghul gaye. Un do boondon ka ab koi alag existence nahi raha tha—wo bhi usi vishal samundar ka hissa ban chuki thi.

Kayi minute tak wo bas waise hi khada raha. Still, calm, completely present. Waves aati rahi, usse takrati rahi, lekin wo gira nahi. Use mehsoos hua ki paani use dubaane nahi, balki usko sambhalne aaya tha.

Suraj ab poori tarah se horizon ke neeche doob chuka tha, aasmaan mein pehla taara chamakne laga tha. 

Kabir ne dheere-dheere apne hath neeche kiye aur apni aankhein kholi. Usne ek aisi gehri saans li jo usne saalon se nahi li thi—clean, salty, and full of life. Chehre par ek aisi muskaan aayi jo dikhawe ki nahi thi. 

Wo mada aur dheere-dheere shore ki taraf wapas chalne laga. Kapde geere the, haath thande the, lekin uske andar ek aag phir se jal chuki thi. Wo toota hua insaan samundar mein hi chhoot gaya tha; kinare par ek naya Kabir laut raha tha.
